Questions, meet answers
What type of account can I invest into?
You can invest with us through a stocks and shares ISA and a general investment account. You can pay into more than one of the same type of ISA in a tax year. But if you already have an existing Cash ISA or Stocks and Shares ISA, you can transfer it to us too and carry on building with us.
Are my investments locked away?
No, you can access your money at any point. But remember, investing is a long-term strategy, so if you think you’ll need your money often, it may be best to put it into an instant access savings account instead.
