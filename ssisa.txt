Questions? We've got answers
How do you open a Stocks & Shares ISA?
First, you need a Monzo current account, then you can open a Stocks & Shares ISA in the Investments area of the app.
How does a Stocks & Shares ISA work?
Stocks & Shares ISAs are a way to invest tax-efficiently, you can invest up to £20,000 each year and any interest you earn will be tax-free.
The taxes you pay depend on your circumstances and could change in the future.
Can I transfer a Cash ISA into a Stocks & Shares ISA?
Yes, you can transfer a Cash ISA into a Stocks & Shares ISA as long as you stick within the £20,000 allowance.
What happens to any income from a Stocks & Shares ISA?
Any income (increases in the value of your investments) that you earn from your Stocks & Shares ISA is tax-free. That means you won’t have to pay tax on it at any time - even if you sell your investments or withdraw the money.
How long should I plan to invest for?
You should plan to invest for at least 5 years.
Am I eligible to apply for a Stocks & Shares ISA?
If you’re a UK resident aged 18+ and you have a Monzo current account, you can apply for a Stocks & Shares ISA.
Can I withdraw money from a Stocks & Shares ISA?
Yes, you can withdraw money at any time.
